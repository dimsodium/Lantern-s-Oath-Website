The Oath

The Wardens of the Emerald Lantern once swore to guard the dark between the stars. Their order has fallen, and only you remain: the last bearer of the ring, whose light burns as bright as your will.

Fly through three trials toward the edge of known space. Each one throws harder enemies at you, wave after wave, until its master shows itself. Blast with the ring, raise shields when the fire gets thick, and when your oath is ready, unleash it.
