Controls

Everything you need to fight your way through all three levels.

Move

Use W, A, S and D to fly your Lantern anywhere on the screen. Your Lantern faces wherever your mouse points, so every attack goes that way, diagonals included.

W A S D

Fly Faster

Hold Shift while you fly with W, A, S and D (or the arrow keys) to boost. Your Lantern flies much faster with a green energy trail, which is great for dodging Otto Zundapt's saws.

SHIFT + W A S D

Forcefield

Hold the space bar to put up a forcefield. It blocks attacks and knocks Otto Zundapt's saw blades away (it deals no damage itself), but every hit cracks it a little more until it shatters. It only drains a little charge while it's up, so you can hold it for a good while.

SPACE (HOLD)

Construct Fist

Press 1 to swing a giant green fist out of your ring. It sweeps a full half circle, from one side, across the front, to the other side, wherever you aim, and hits every rat it passes through. Costs a little ring charge.

1

Construct Boulder

Press 2 to launch a big glowing boulder where you're aiming. It smashes through every enemy in its path, but it costs a big chunk of your ring charge and needs 2 seconds to recharge.

2

Ring Beam

Hold 3 to fire a beam straight out of your lantern ring. It hits everything in its path and drains charge while you hold it.

3 (HOLD)

Construct Rope

Press 4 to whirl a green lasso construct and throw it at up to four of the closest enemies. A loop drops over each one and cinches tight, then the caught rats are reeled into the middle, where they stay tied up for a moment. With no rats around, it ties up one boss (Otto Zundapt or a single Manhunter) right where he stands: he's held for 2 seconds, or up to 3 if you keep 4 held down, and breaks free the moment you let go after that. The lasso does no damage, but you can hit him while he's tied up. Costs a lot of charge and needs 3 seconds to recharge.

4

Punch

Press C for a quick basic punch. It's weaker than a construct, but it costs no ring charge, so you can always fight back.

C

Beware My Power

When the bar under your ring charge is full and your ring is fully charged, press V. Your Lantern unleashes a huge energy burst that wipes out every enemy on screen and takes 65% of a mini-boss's health, but it uses up all of your ring's charge, so recharge with G afterwards.

V

Recharge

Your ring doesn't refill on its own. Tap G and your Lantern holds up his power battery and recites the Lantern's Oath, filling the ring all the way over 6 seconds (tap G again to stop early). For a quick top-up, hold G instead and it charges for as long as you hold it. A see-through lantern construct forms around you while you recharge and blocks 3 hits before it shatters. You can't move or use constructs while recharging, and a hit after the lantern breaks knocks you out of it, except when you start the Oath after powering down to human form: then nothing can hurt you until the Oath is finished.

G / HOLD G

Pause

Press P (or Esc) or click the pause button in the top right corner to pause. The pause menu has Options (resume, restart or quit), Controls and Settings. Press P again, or click outside the buttons, to jump back into the fight.

P

How a run works

  • Your Lantern has one life. The ring in the bottom left is that life, and the red bar next to the heart is your health.
  • The green bar in the top left is your ring's charge. Every construct uses some, and it only refills when you recharge from your lantern with G. If it runs completely dry while your health is at half or lower, your Lantern loses his suit and turns back into plain Hal, on foot with no flying boost, until you recharge.
  • Level 1 starts with a short tutorial: move, face with the cursor, fly fast, then each construct on a few rats sent in to try it on, and finally Beware My Power on eight rats closing in from every side. Press Enter to skip it.
  • Space rats keep coming in from both sides, and more of them show up the higher you fly. Every rat you take down lets you climb further, until you reach Otto Zundapt.
  • At the top of the level, Otto Zundapt is waiting. He flies all over the arena and throws spinning saws like a frisbee. Boost with Shift to dodge them, and keep your forcefield ready: his goggle laser locks on and only the forcefield stops it.
  • Beat Otto Zundapt and the fight moves to the desert for Level 2, where your attacks hit 35% harder. Two Manhunters, Zephelone and Asmodius, take turns rushing in for a punch to the body, throw sand tornadoes that lock on and chase you once you get close (dodge one and it drifts off into the distance), and fire pairs of red and green laser shots. Your forcefield blocks all of it, though a tornado takes half its strength. Hold your ring beam on a tornado for 4 seconds and it turns green and flies off along the beam, hitting any Manhunter in its path. When one rushes you, a three-pip guard bar shows over his head: land three hits before he connects and he drops to one knee, stunned for 3 seconds. Defeat both to clear the level.
  • Your Lantern uses one ability at a time. A punch, construct, beam or forcefield has to finish (or be let go) before the next one starts, and recharging and Beware My Power wait for it too.
  • On a phone or tablet, drag on the left side to fly and use the buttons on the right for constructs.
